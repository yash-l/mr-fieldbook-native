# MR One v1.4.3

- Nearby hospitals no longer requires Google Places.
- Added user-triggered OpenStreetMap Overpass search for hospital, clinic and doctor-office POIs within 500 m / 1 km / 2 km / 5 km.
- Nearby OSM results are cached locally for 24 hours and can be linked to the accurate saved doctor.
- Existing saved pins remain available offline.
- Google Places remains optional as a secondary search when configured.
- OSM and Google IDs are stored separately; existing doctor data is not overwritten until a hospital and doctor are explicitly selected.
